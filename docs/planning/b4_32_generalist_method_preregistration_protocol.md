## Independent derivation and exact assumptions

The primary paper by Peeters, Hong and Abdalla (2018),
[A compliance approximation method applied to variable stiffness composite optimisation](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00158-018-2007-2),
sections3–4/equations19–25, motivates fixed admissible forces and reciprocal
stiffness through complementary energy. Its composite/truss results are not
TopoLab evidence. No code, figures, optimizer, damping rule or reported speed
is imported. The following discrete scalar-SIMP argument is independently
derived from TopoLab's frozen equations, including its full compliance rather
than half strain energy. No Hack3D content is consulted or copied.

Let R_e restrict free displacements to an element, including homogeneous
zero prescribed displacements, and K0_e be its symmetric positive-semidefinite
unit-modulus matrix. Hold load, support, mesh and element space fixed. Require
positive E_e(rho)=E_min+(E_0-E_min)*rho_e**p and assembled free K(rho) positive
definite. For an EXACT equilibrium anchor K(rho_star)u_star=f, define

    q_star_e = u_star_e.T K0_e u_star_e
    a_e = E_star_e**2 * q_star_e >= 0
    U(rho) = sum_e a_e / E_e(rho).

For any free v, exact anchor equilibrium gives

    f.T v = sum_e E_star_e * u_star_e.T K0_e v_e
    (f.T v)**2 <= U(rho) * v.T K(rho) v.

The second line follows from weighted Cauchy–Schwarz on the PSD element
seminorms; it needs no inverse of singular element matrices. At v=K(rho)^-1 f,
nonzero f gives C(rho)>0 and hence C(rho)<=U(rho). Equality holds at the anchor.
The exact gap also has the independently checkable identity

    U(rho)-C(rho) = sum_e E_e(rho) * d_e.T K0_e d_e
    d_e = (E_star_e/E_e(rho))*u_star_e - u_e(rho).

The prospective physical derivative is

    dU/drho_e = -a_e*p*(E_0-E_min)*rho_e**(p-1)/E_e(rho)**2.

At the anchor it equals the existing signed SIMP compliance derivative.
The coefficient is element energy, not a clipped positive design-sensitivity
weight. Filter/volume pullback would retain the existing conventions and v3
map; no projection evaluation/integration occurs here or in proposed B4.33.
Stored C_s and continuous C_star stay distinct: normalized value is U/C_s,
with anchor intercept C_star/C_s, not an imposed one. Do not differentiate
stored quantization, detach a volume offset or choose a normalizer to pass.

These conditional identities do not establish convexity in SIMP density,
surrogate tightness away from the anchor, fitted descent, terminal reliability,
global optimizer convergence, feasible cost or P17 repair. One fixed anchor
is preregistered; no refresh schedule, multiple-anchor envelope, damping/loss
coefficient, trust radius or search is selected.

## Floating equilibrium is a separate blocking obligation

alone does not make its element forces statically admissible and does not

    C(rho) <= (sqrt(U_tilde(rho)) + norm(r,2)/sqrt(m_rho))**2.
